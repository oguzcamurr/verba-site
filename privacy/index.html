Verba

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 7, 2026

Verba does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no tracking, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your levels, languages, settings, and the words you have seen, saved or marked as known are stored on your iPhone, so the app and its widgets can show your next word. They are included in your normal device backup and are never sent to us.

iCloud Backup

Unless you turn it off in Settings, Verba keeps a copy of your progress and settings in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone can restore it. Apple stores it under your account; we can't see it. You can also save a backup as a file, which goes only where you put it.

Purchases

Verba Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Pronunciation

When you tap the speaker, the word is spoken by your iPhone's built-in voice. Nothing is recorded and no audio leaves your device.

Words you add

Words you add yourself, including words you share to Verba from other apps, stay on your iPhone with the rest of your progress, and in your own iCloud backup if it's on. They are never sent to us.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your iPhone schedules it. Nothing about it reaches us or anyone else.

Sharing

When you share a word, your progress or a game result, the image is made on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to send it.

Your voice

Say it back records your voice so you can hear it next to the spoken sentence. The recording stays on your iPhone, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you move on. If you ask what the phone heard, your iPhone works it out itself; the audio isn't sent anywhere.

Children

Verba collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
