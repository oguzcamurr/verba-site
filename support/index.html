Support · One useful word at a time, on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Add the Home Screen widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Verba, pick a size, and tap Add Widget.

Add the Lock Screen widget

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area (or the date above the clock) and choose Verba.

My widget doesn't change

Words change on the schedule you choose in Settings (from every 30 minutes to once a day). iOS decides the exact moment widgets refresh, so a change can arrive a little after the hour. Opening Verba refreshes the widget immediately.

Meanings in your language

Verba shows a short meaning in Turkish, Spanish, German or French, following your iPhone's language. Change it in Settings → Meanings, or choose None. This is free.

Learn another language (Pro)

With Verba Pro you can learn Spanish, German, French or Turkish words instead of English: Settings → Learning. Each language keeps its own level and progress.

Restore Verba Pro

Open Verba → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.
